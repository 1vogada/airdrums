## What they disagreed on

- Front door: is Home itself the song-select screen, with the search box focused and results replacing the shelves in place (Game designer, Audio, Player, QA), or a dashboard of cards and shelves with search results on their own route (Teacher, UX, Architect)? And what sits first on it: the Continue / Learning card (Teacher) or the Now-on-Spotify card (UX, Architect, Player)?
- After picking a search result: does Enter start the song at once for a ten-second path to notes (Game designer, Player), or open a song page first so difficulty, version and parts are seen before playing (Teacher, UX, Architect, Audio, QA)?
- Song hub: a full Song page on its own route (Teacher, UX, Architect, Audio, QA), a side panel on the Songs screen (Game designer), or no song page and a Ready card over the play screen with version and level in a Tune panel (Player)?
- URLs: is a song addressed by chart md5 (Game designer, UX, Architect) or by an artist|title key (Teacher, Audio, QA)? And do the old #/tab ... #/mania links redirect to a new play route (the other six) or stay as the play screen's real addresses, with practice at #/tab/practice and results at #/results (QA)?
- Default 'Play' when Spotify is linked: start or follow Spotify (Game designer: Sound defaults to Spotify; Player) or run the site's own clock with guide drums, Spotify being a separate named button (Architect, Audio, QA)?
- Starting the song on the user's Spotify from the site (search + devices + play endpoints): build it now with designed fallbacks (Player: must; Game designer, UX, Audio: should; Teacher) or ship only 'start it in Spotify yourself, the notes follow' and add remote start in a later release, because it is Premium-only and cannot be tested (Architect, QA)?
- Spotify Liked Songs import: build an opt-in, slow, cached version now (Player: should; Teacher, Game designer, UX: could, last) or leave it out because it needs a new permission and re-link and spends the unpublished quota the working follow mode depends on (Architect, Audio, QA)? If built: per visible row only, 50 at a time (Teacher, Game designer, Architect's fallback), 100 per button press (UX), or 200 checked in the background over about 5 minutes and resumed across visits (Player)?
- Depth of Spotify changes before the owner has tried a release: rewrite the request helper and polling now as a must (Architect, Audio) or change only the wait between asks plus the expired-login and quota handling, leaving steady() alone (QA)? During a quota pause, do the notes coast on the last known position (Audio) or does the site stop and offer its own clock (Architect, QA)?
- Part jumps while following Spotify: one Spotify seek per click (Teacher, UX, Architect, Audio, Player) or part clicks work on the site's own clock only (Game designer, QA)?
- Entering Learn while Spotify is playing: send Spotify a pause command (UX, Player) or only tell the user to pause it and make no call (Teacher, Architect, Audio)?
- Automation in learning: do tempo steps and the move to the next part happen by themselves because the learner's hands are full (Teacher, Game designer, UX, Audio, Player; Architect for tempo only), or only on the player's own click because every automatic path adds states to test (QA; Architect and Teacher for the part advance)?
- Auto-tempo thresholds: step up after one pass at 85% (UX, Player), one at 90% (Game designer), two in a row at 85% (Teacher, Audio) or two in a row at 90% with a miss cap (Architect)? Step down after two passes under 60% (Game designer, Architect), two under 50% (Audio, Player), three under 50% (UX) or three under 60% followed by halving the loop (Teacher)?
- Is a part 'learned' after one clean pass at 100% with all drums (Game designer, UX, Architect, Audio, Player, QA) or only after the top rung is clean twice (Teacher)? And can the learner mark it learned by hand when the site cannot hear them (Teacher: G / Shift+G always wins over the mic; UX; Player: outlined tick) or only through a judged pass (Game designer, Architect, Audio, QA)?
- Listen pass: automatic on the first pass of a new part (Game designer, UX, Player), automatic after every change of part, tempo or layer (Teacher, Audio), alternating with every judged pass when the mic is on speakers (Architect), or never automatic, two buttons Listen and Play (QA)?
- What may the site sound while the mic is scoring through speakers: nothing at all (QA, Architect), the count-in click only (Game designer, Audio), a quiet click throughout (Teacher, Player), or click plus the guide playing the drums the learner is not playing (UX)?
- Mic scoring on the site's own clock before the kit-to-mic delay is known: judge anyway and correct it automatically per pass (Teacher), with a measure / centre tool starting from 0 (Audio), or by a manual nudge and the results-screen fix (Game designer, UX, Player), or do not judge mic hits at all until a calibration has been done in Setup (QA)?
- In Learn, how is a pass scored: share of notes hit with a wrong-drum guess from the mic counting as a hit (Teacher) or the normal judging rules and points over the maximum (Game designer, Player's step list, QA)?
- Layers: three presets (Teacher, Game designer, Architect, Player, QA), four (Audio), or All / Feet + snare / Hands with per-drum toggles and site-made 'Basics' levels (UX)? Does a new part start on bass + snare (Teacher, Player, Audio) or on all drums unless the part is busy (Architect) or with only a suggestion (UX)? Do layers advance by themselves (Teacher's six-rung ladder; Player: next layer at 80% after clearing 100%) or stay a manual choice (Game designer, UX, Architect, Audio, QA)? Are layers offered while following Spotify (Teacher) or only on the site's clock and menu?
- Loop unit: whole parts only (the other six) or also 4, 2 and 1 bar windows inside a part (Teacher)? Loops over a run of parts: yes (six members) or not in this version (QA)? Charts with three or fewer sections: cut into 8-bar blocks (Game designer, Architect, Player) or left as long loops (QA)?
- Short parts: merge under 6 s (Game designer, UX, Audio, Player), under 6 s or 8 notes (Architect, QA) or under 2 bars (Teacher); into the part before (Teacher, Game designer, QA) or the next (UX, Player)? Is part progress keyed by md5 + section index (Game designer, Architect) or by index + start time so it survives renames (QA)?
- Count-in: before every pass (Teacher, Game designer, UX, Audio, Player, QA) or only before the first pass, later passes looping straight round (Architect)? Numerals counting up 1-2-3-4 (Teacher, Game designer) or down 4-3-2-1 (UX, Architect, Player, QA)?
- Wait-for-me step mode: include as an optional extra (UX; Audio: could; Player: could) or cut because a clock that stops teaches stopping and adds an untestable clock state (Teacher, Game designer, Architect)?
- Learning mode in which views: Tab, Drums and Colors only, with Taiko and Mania left as Play views (Teacher), or all five views in practice (QA and the others)?
- render.js: not edited at all in this version, with the part strip and count-in as HTML over the canvas (QA), small additions only (Architect: setMode, section names, one overlay), or real drawing changes (UX: lyrics and HUD inside the canvas, note letters and shapes; Game designer: note speed; Teacher: count row and loop brackets; Audio: dimming outside the loop in all five views)?
- tests/ear_e2e.mjs: must it pass unchanged as part of the ship gate (QA, Architect) or must it be updated in the same sitting because the own clock moves the lyric shift from the notes to the lyrics and so changes game.offset on the demo link (Audio)? Related: is line-up by ear off on the own clock always (Audio, QA) or allowed when the site is silent (Architect)?
- Test input: fake keys sent from outside the page with no test code in production (Architect), a robot drummer inside the page (QA), or a shipped ?bot= autoplay hook shared with the Listen demo (Game designer)?
- Storage: split the library into an index plus one entry per song with a schema number (Architect) or keep the single library key that js/library.js writes today and only add keys (QA)?
- IndexedDB cache for charts and cover thumbnails: build it (Architect: should, storing file bytes; Player: should, storing parsed charts; Game designer: could; Audio) or cut it as a second storage layer with its own failure modes (QA)?
- Album art in result lists: lazy-loaded chart-site pictures on every row (Teacher, Game designer, UX, Architect, Audio, Player) or none, with coloured tiles and one picture on the Song page only (QA)?
- Starter content on Home: shelves fetched from the chart site's advanced search (one: Game designer, Architect, Player; two: UX, Audio) or no fetched shelves, only a hand-picked list in the code (Teacher: 12 with reasons; QA: 8 chips)? Game designer proposes both a 24-title list and one shelf.
- Synth fix: render every voice once to a buffer with fixed peaks (Audio, Architect) or keep the live synth, lower the guide bus and add an upper layer to the bass drum (QA, with Teacher and Game designer naming the bus level only)?
- Where Sync and Mic live on the play screen: an always-visible chip with the raw millisecond value (Game designer, Architect, QA), a chip in words (UX), status dots with the number hidden in a Tune panel (Player), inside the '...' drawer with only a status pill (Teacher), or in the bottom dock (Audio)?
- Lyrics and score: drawn inside the canvas corners to free about 76 px (UX) or kept as a strip outside the canvas (Game designer, Architect, Audio, Player, QA)? Is the biggest number accuracy % (Game designer) or score (UX, Player)?
- Part timeline: under the header (Teacher, Game designer, Architect, Audio, Player), inside the bottom transport (UX) or under the lyrics strip (QA)? Coloured by learning state (Teacher, Architect, Player in Practise), by this run's accuracy as parts are passed (Game designer) or by kind of part (Player)?
- Results: an overlay on the play screen (Game designer, UX, Architect, Audio, Player), a replacement of the stage (Teacher) or a separate #/results route (QA)? A results view after a Spotify-followed song (Game designer, UX, Architect, Audio, Player) or only a toast (QA)?
- Timing feedback: histograms and a live hit-error meter (Game designer, Audio), two figures Tightness and Lean (Teacher), one sentence with or without a fix button (UX, Architect, Player) or none in this version (QA)? Per-drum accuracy on results (UX, Audio) or not (Player)?
- Key map: view switch on 1-5 (UX, Player) or V (Game designer, Architect, who keep the number row clear or use 1 2 3 for layers, as Teacher does)? Like on F (Teacher, Player) or H (Game designer, UX, Architect)? Tempo on Up/Down (Teacher, Audio, Player, QA) or - and = (Game designer, UX, Architect)? Left/Right as 5 s seek (Game designer, UX, Architect) or as part / loop-window keys (Teacher, Audio, Player)? Restart on Home (Teacher, UX, Architect, Audio) or R (Player; Game designer on overlays only)? Esc as pause overlay (Game designer) or back (UX, Architect, Player)?
- Drum test keys: always live, with Space staying the bass drum (UX, QA, today's behaviour), or live only when the mic is off or a 'Keyboard drums' switch is on, with Space as start/pause when the mic is on (Player)?
- Hands-free start: four hits within 2.5 s (UX), any single hit after 1 s of quiet (Player), or no hit-to-start (the other five do not propose it)? Should the chrome fade while playing (UX to 35% after 4 s; Player fully after 3 s) or stay put (the others)?
- Difficulty on a result row: tier dots (Teacher 5 plus notes per second, Game designer 7, Architect 0-6), a 'how busy' meter that avoids the words Easy/Hard (UX), one word Easy / Medium / Hard / Brutal (Player) or a 'Drums 3/6' chip (QA)?

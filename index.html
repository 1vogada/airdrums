Kit map

Six zones in the air in front of you, two rows of three, and one pedal under each foot. The LED on the stick shows the colour of the zone it's pointing at.

  • Red LED means the stick is out of range. Bring it back into one of the six zones.
  • Left foot is the hi-hat pedal. Putting it down plays the hi-hat. Where it rests changes the stick's Hi-Hat zone: foot flat is a closed hi-hat, foot raised is an open one.
  • Right foot is the bass drum. Raise it, then put it down flat.
  • On the play screen the Drums view uses this same layout. The Colors, Taiko and Mania views use the LED colours.

Setup

1. Spotify

Spotify only lets a hobby app have a few users, so you use your own free app (the one you made for minitype works).

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and open your app (or create one, Web API).

  3. Paste the app's client ID here:

2. Microphone

The sticks only talk to the speaker, so the site listens for your hits. Put the mic near the Air Drums speaker.

Hit a drum to see how loud it comes in.

Music from the same speaker also reaches the mic. Turn the music down until it stays under the line and only your hits go over it, or play the music on headphones.

3. Teach it your drums (optional)

Without this it scores your timing. With it, it also checks you hit the right drum. Pick a drum, then hit it 4 times.

4. Sound (for Play and Learn without Spotify)

Play and Learn use the site's own drum sounds and a click, made in your browser. Nothing is downloaded.

You should hear a bass drum, a snare, hi-hats, two toms, a crash and a ride.

If the microphone is on and hears the site's drums through your speakers, it counts them as your hits. Use headphones, or turn Guide off while you want a score.

Not tried on a real kit yet

Scoring by microphone, line-up by ear, the Spotify link and the Learn mode's scoring were only tested with stand-ins: a recording as the microphone, the keyboard as the drums. If something is off with your kit, that is the first place to look. The previous version is still at v1.
